Project Overview

An Address of Elegance in Sector 10, Greater Noida West

Embassy Autograph is a gated community brought to life by Saraswati Group of Housing in Sector 10, Greater Noida West — an address shaped around cleaner-air living and quiet luxury.

Seven premium towers rise four-side open, offering 225+ apartments within a high security zone protected by CCTV cameras & video surveillance and boom barrier car parking. Everyday life is eased by grocery stores in every tower and a waiting lounge in every tower, while the 4,000 sq. ft. standalone clubhouse — Club Elegance — anchors the community.
